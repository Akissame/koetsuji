FAQ

Good to know.

Straightforward answers about your vault, backups and the way Aegis works.

Does Aegis store my data in the cloud?

No. Your vault is stored locally on your device. Aegis does not provide an account-based cloud vault or automatic cloud synchronisation.

What happens if I forget my master password?

Your master password is essential to access your vault. There is no password-reset service that can recover it for you. Keep a secure backup and make sure you can remember your master password before relying on Aegis as your only copy.

Can I move my vault to another device?

Use Aegis's backup and restore options to transfer your vault. Keep the backup file somewhere secure and follow the app's instructions when importing it on the new device.

Does Aegis work without an internet connection?

The core vault is designed for offline use. Internet access may still be needed for store services and, in the Lite edition, advertising.

What is Emergency Mode?

Emergency Mode is an additional privacy feature available in the paid edition. Review its in-app setup instructions carefully before enabling it, so you understand how to activate and deactivate it.

Are Aegis Lite and Aegis the same app?

They are separate editions. Aegis Lite is free and ad-supported; the paid Aegis edition removes advertising and includes the additional features listed above. Check each store listing for platform-specific availability.

Does Aegis have access to my fingerprint or Face ID data?

Biometric verification is handled through the device's operating-system authentication features. Aegis does not need access to raw fingerprint or face images.

What is the Digital Testament?

The Digital Testament is an optional feature in the paid edition intended to help you prepare instructions and credential information for trusted beneficiaries. Read its in-app explanation carefully and plan how the generated document will be stored and shared.

Privacy

Your vault stays yours.

A concise overview of how Aegis handles information. The details can vary between the Lite and paid editions and between platform providers.

Questions? Contact Aegis support.

Vault data and local storage

Credentials and other information you save in Aegis are stored in an encrypted vault on your device. Aegis does not operate a cloud vault or account service for storing or synchronising that information.

Encryption and access

Your vault is protected by the app's encryption and authentication mechanisms. Keep your master password private, use a strong password and maintain a secure backup. Do not rely on this summary as a substitute for the technical and security information supplied with the current app version.

Biometric authentication

If you enable biometric unlock, authentication is performed using the operating system's supported biometric facilities. Aegis does not receive raw fingerprint or face images.

Advertising in Aegis Lite

The Lite edition displays ads through Google AdMob. Advertising providers may process device or advertising information under their own policies and settings. The paid Aegis edition is intended to provide an ad-free experience.

Google Privacy Policy.

Backups and exported files

When you create a backup, you control where the resulting file is saved or shared. Protect backup files as carefully as your master password and avoid sending them through channels you do not trust.

Permissions and device data

Aegis may request device permissions to support features you choose to use. Permission availability and platform disclosures can differ between Android and iOS. Review the permission prompts and the applicable store privacy disclosures on your device.

Children's privacy

Aegis is a general-purpose password manager and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians should review the applicable store age ratings and platform privacy information.

Policy updates and contact

This information may be updated as Aegis changes. For questions about the app or this privacy summary, email aegis@akissame.com.co.
