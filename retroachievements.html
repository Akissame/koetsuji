Good to know

Questions, answered.

A few things that can help you get started with RetroAchievements and the companion app.

Independent project. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by RetroAchievements.org. It uses data made available through the service's public API.
Do I need a RetroAchievements account?

Yes. You need a free account on RetroAchievements.org to use the service and its features.

Does the app unlock achievements while I play?

No. Achievement unlocking takes place through a compatible emulator connected to your RetroAchievements account. This app is a companion for browsing your profile, progress and community activity.

How do I enable RetroAchievements in an emulator?
  • Open the RetroAchievements settings in a supported emulator, such as RetroArch.
  • Enter your RetroAchievements username and password.
  • Enable the RetroAchievements option.
  • Enable Hardcore mode if you want eligible achievements to count toward Hardcore rankings.
  • Restart the emulator if the settings require it.
What is Hardcore mode?

Hardcore mode is the stricter way to earn achievements. It disables features such as save states, rewind and cheats while playing, and enables Hardcore points and leaderboard participation where supported.

Why are my points or achievements not showing?

Check that your emulator is connected to the correct account, Hardcore mode is enabled when required, the game is recognized correctly and your connection is working. Also make sure your emulator is up to date.

Is the app free?

The app is free to download and includes advertising. An optional subscription is available to remove ads. Check the store listing for the current offer and terms.

Where can I read the privacy information?

The app uses data from the public RetroAchievements API. Read the RetroAchievements service terms and privacy information, and the app's applicable store disclosures.
